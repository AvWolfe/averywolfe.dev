Enemy AI traits

Every unit in the game runs the same planner. It scores the moves available to it, the lines of sight those moves open or close, and the abilities it could spend energy on, then takes the best option and repeats until the turn is done.

Traits don't add special cases to that loop. They change the weights inside it — how much a kill is worth, how badly it wants to stay unseen, what it will pay to close distance. Two units with different traits read an identical board and come to opposite conclusions, which is the whole point: an encounter should not feel like one enemy copy-pasted.
